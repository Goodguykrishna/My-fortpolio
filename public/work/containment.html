Ⅳ · Containment · a loop

Something is
in there.

The glass is holding something, and it does not want to be held. Press on it. It will strain, then craze, then fail — and what comes out bends the light around it, and the wreckage with it.

One control Hold the cube  it strains, then fails
Drag  orbit the singularity
Wait  it eats everything
Tap the bowl  break it
Or wait  it mends itself in gold
Reset  start the loop over
What it was holding The singularity is integrated,
not painted: every photon is
bent step by step through a
Schwarzschild field. The disk
wraps overhead because the
light from behind is lensed
over the top. The bright limb
is the side rotating toward
you — Doppler beaming.
