Ⅰ · Chawan

The break is
not the flaw.

Kintsugi mends broken pottery with lacquer and gold — never hiding the fracture, but tracing it. The bowl leaves the repair worth more than it was whole.

Interact Drag  turn the bowl
Tap  it cracks. tap again, harder
Hold  a heavier strike
Hover a piece  gold reaches for it
Notes Gold only bridges to what
is still standing. Rebuild
outward from the body.

The chips never come back.
